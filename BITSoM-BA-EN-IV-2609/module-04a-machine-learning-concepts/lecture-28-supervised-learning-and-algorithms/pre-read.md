## Learning objectives

- This lecture dives deeper into supervised learning techniques
- It explains regression methods such as Linear Regression for predicting continuous values and classification methods such as Logistic Regression and Decision Trees for assigning categories
- The concepts of underfitting, overfitting, and the bias–variance tradeoff are introduced, along with visual demonstrations of model learning
- The lecture also covers evaluation metrics, including Mean Squared Error (MSE) and R-squared for regression, and accuracy, confusion matrix, precision, recall, and F1-score for classification
- Emphasis is placed on choosing appropriate metrics and the importance of proper model evaluation
