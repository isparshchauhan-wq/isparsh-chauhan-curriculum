## Learning objectives

- Understand what the minimum of a curve represents and why the slope is zero at the minimum
- Understand how the gradient indicates the direction of steepest increase
- Use the gradient to determine the direction in which to move to minimise a function
- Understand how gradient descent takes small steps towards the minimum
- Understand the role of the learning rate and why steps that are too large or too small can cause problems
